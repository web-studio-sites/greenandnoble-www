Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how Green & Noble Ltd (“Green & Noble”, “we”, “us”) collects and uses your personal information when you visit our website, contact us, request a quotation or use our services. We are committed to protecting your information and handling it in line with UK data protection law, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who we are

Green & Noble Ltd is the controller of your personal information.

  • Company number: [to be supplied]
  • Registered office: [to be supplied]
  • Email: enquiries@greenandnoble.co.uk
  • Telephone: 07502 047000

The information we collect

When you request a quotation or contact us, through our enquiry form, by email, by telephone or on WhatsApp, we collect the details you give us. This usually includes your name, email address, telephone number, the location of the property, the service you are interested in, your message and any photographs you choose to send.

When we provide our services, we may also hold the property address, access arrangements, details of the work agreed, quotations, invoices and payment records. If you are an estate agent, letting agent, landlord or property manager, we may hold your business contact details and those of the people we need to liaise with.

When you visit our website, our hosting provider records basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to keep the website running securely. With your permission, we also use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website (see “Cookies and analytics” below).

Photographs you send of your garden may show your property. We only use them to understand and quote for the work. We will not publish photographs of your property on our website or social media without your permission.

How and why we use your information

We use your information to:

  • respond to your enquiry and prepare a quotation (we do this to take steps at your request before entering into a contract)
  • arrange, carry out and invoice the work you ask us to do (to perform our contract with you)
  • keep business and accounting records (to meet our legal obligations)
  • keep our website secure and understand how it is used, so we can improve it (our legitimate interests, and your consent for analytics cookies)

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for automated decision-making.

Who we share it with

We only share your information where needed to run our business, with trusted providers who process it on our behalf and under our instructions, including:

  • our email and office software provider (Google Workspace)
  • our website hosting provider and the service that delivers enquiry form submissions to us
  • Google Analytics, only if you accept analytics cookies
  • WhatsApp (Meta), if you choose to contact us that way
  • our accountant, and specialist partners (such as our tree surgeon or trade partner) where they are carrying out part of the work you have asked for

We may also share information where the law requires us to.

Some of these providers are based outside the UK, for example in the United States. Where this happens, your information is protected by UK adequacy regulations or by approved contractual safeguards.

Cookies and analytics

Our website only sets analytics cookies if you click Accept on the cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page. Our Cookie Policy explains this in more detail.

Our website also loads its fonts from Google Fonts. To do this, your browser connects to Google, which receives your IP address. Google Fonts does not set cookies.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and quotations that do not lead to work: up to 12 months, in case you come back to us.
  • Customer records, quotations and invoices: 6 years after the end of the financial year in which the work was completed, as required for tax and accounting purposes.
  • Website analytics: up to 14 months.

After this, we securely delete or anonymise your information.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • ask us to delete your information
  • object to, or ask us to restrict, how we use your information
  • withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent
  • ask us to transfer your information to another organisation, where this applies

To make a request, please email enquiries@greenandnoble.co.uk. We will respond within one month.

Keeping your information safe

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including secure, password-protected systems and limiting access to the people who need it.

Complaints

If you have a concern about how we use your information, please contact us first and we will do our best to resolve it. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
