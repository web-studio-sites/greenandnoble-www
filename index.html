Our Story · Meet the Founders

A Passion for Gardens. A Personal Commitment to Excellence.

Green & Noble was founded by Jason Cooper and Richard Maybey with a shared passion for gardens and outdoor spaces, and a belief that customers deserve more than simply a job well done. They deserve a professional, dependable service with a genuinely personal touch.

At the heart of Green & Noble is our customer-first approach. We believe the best results always begin with listening. By taking the time to understand what each customer wants to achieve, we can offer honest, knowledgeable advice, bring fresh ideas and creativity, and develop practical solutions that suit their garden, lifestyle and budget.

Our promise is simple: to listen first, offer thoughtful and knowledgeable advice, and deliver on our commitments, bringing our customers' gardens to life with a standard of workmanship and personal service they can trust.
