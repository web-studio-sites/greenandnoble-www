Cookie Policy

This cookie policy explains how Green & Noble Ltd (“we”, “us”) uses cookies and similar technologies on this website. Please read it alongside our Privacy Policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device when you visit. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, and to give the website owner information about how the site is used.

Your choice

When you first visit our website, a banner asks whether you accept analytics cookies. Nothing that sets cookies is loaded until you choose. If you click Reject, no analytics or third-party cookies are set.

You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page.

We remember your choice in your browser’s local storage, so we don’t ask again on every page. This is not a cookie and is not shared with anyone.

Cookies we use

With your permission, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website, so we can improve it. It sets these cookies:

  • _ga: distinguishes visitors. Expires after 2 years.
  • ga[ID]: keeps track of your visit. Expires after 2 years.

These are only set if you click Accept, and are removed if you later change your choice to Reject.

We do not use advertising cookies.

Other services

  • Google Fonts: our website’s fonts are loaded from Google, which receives your device’s IP address. Google Fonts does not set cookies.
  • Links to Google, Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp: these sites set their own cookies if you follow a link to them. Their own cookie policies apply.

Managing cookies in your browser

You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings. For help, visit aboutcookies.org.
