First Impressions Begin Before the Front Door

At Green & Noble, we understand the importance of presenting a property at its best, particularly when preparing it for sale, letting or the arrival of new occupants.

Gardens and outdoor spaces contribute significantly to a property's overall appearance. Overgrown shrubs, tired lawns, neglected borders and poorly maintained patios can detract from an otherwise attractive home.

We provide professional garden presentation, restoration and improvement services designed to help homeowners and property professionals prepare outdoor spaces efficiently, thoughtfully and to a high standard.

Working across Surrey and London, we support private homeowners, estate agents, letting agents, landlords and property managers with everything from targeted garden enhancements to more substantial garden resets.

Our approach combines practical horticultural knowledge, careful workmanship and the professional standards and customer service that define Green & Noble.

Helping Properties Make the Right First Impression

Pre-Sale Garden Presentation

When preparing a home for sale, the garden should complement the property and help prospective buyers appreciate the outdoor space.

Green & Noble provides targeted garden improvements ahead of marketing photography, viewings and property launches.

We assess the existing garden and recommend practical enhancements that can make a noticeable visual difference, taking into account the property's character, available budget and marketing timescale.

Our pre-sale services include

  • Complete garden presentation and tidy-ups
  • Restoration of neglected borders and planting areas
  • Hedge and shrub shaping
  • Ornamental tree management where appropriate
  • Lawn improvement and renovation
  • Seasonal planting and colour
  • Decorative stone and gravel refreshes
  • Patio, pathway and driveway cleaning
  • Weed and vegetation removal
  • Green waste clearance
  • Targeted garden enhancements
  • Ongoing care while a property remains on the market

Our focus is on identifying improvements that enhance presentation without recommending unnecessary expenditure or extensive redesign where simpler solutions will achieve the desired result.

Whether preparing a small courtyard garden or a larger established outdoor space, we take a practical approach to helping the property look its best.

Restoring Gardens Between Tenancies

End-of-Tenancy Garden Resets

Outdoor spaces can deteriorate during a tenancy, particularly where regular maintenance has been overlooked.

Overgrown lawns, unmanaged hedges, weeds and neglected planting can leave a garden requiring more than a routine tidy-up before a property is ready for its next occupants.

Green & Noble provides structured end-of-tenancy garden resets to restore outdoor areas to a clean, manageable and presentable condition.

Our garden reset services include

  • Overgrown garden clearance
  • Grass cutting and lawn restoration
  • Hedge and shrub reductions
  • Border clearance and redefinition
  • Weed and bramble removal
  • Appropriate pruning of established plants
  • Removal of unwanted vegetation
  • Patio and pathway cleaning
  • Green waste removal and responsible disposal
  • Preparation for new turf or planting where required
  • General garden presentation before re-letting

We assess each garden individually and provide a clear written quotation outlining the proposed work.

Where appropriate, we aim to retain healthy, established planting rather than unnecessarily removing valuable garden features.

Our objective is to return the outdoor space to a suitable condition for re-letting and future maintenance.

Estate Agents & Letting Agents

We recognise that estate agents and letting agents frequently work to demanding timescales, particularly when preparing properties for photography, marketing, viewings or new tenancies.

Green & Noble offers a professional, straightforward service, with clear communication and a practical understanding of property presentation requirements.

We can assist with

  • Pre-marketing garden assessments
  • One-off property presentation projects
  • Garden resets between tenancies
  • Urgent garden tidy-ups, subject to availability
  • Lawn and planting improvements
  • Ongoing care for vacant or marketed properties

Our aim is to build trusted, long-term relationships with estate and letting agents who value reliable communication, consistent standards and a professional approach.

Whether you have an individual property requiring attention or regularly manage properties across Surrey and London, we would welcome the opportunity to discuss how Green & Noble can support your requirements.

Protecting the Appearance of Outdoor Spaces

Landlords & Property Managers

Maintaining gardens and exterior spaces is an important consideration for landlords and residential property managers.

Green & Noble provides both one-off restoration services and planned garden maintenance arrangements to help keep outdoor areas attractive, manageable and appropriately maintained.

Our services include

  • Vacant-property garden care
  • Pre-tenancy garden preparation
  • End-of-tenancy garden restoration
  • Scheduled residential garden maintenance
  • Hedge and shrub management
  • Lawn care and seasonal improvements
  • Replacement planting
  • Low-maintenance garden solutions
  • Communal garden and outdoor area care, subject to site requirements

For properties with repeated maintenance challenges, we can also recommend practical improvements designed to make the garden easier to manage over time.

Practical Improvements Without Unnecessary Expense

Garden Enhancements That Make a Difference

Not every property requires a major landscaping project.

Often, carefully chosen enhancements can improve the appearance of an outdoor space significantly.

Depending on the property, we may recommend

  • Restoring existing lawns rather than replacing them
  • Introducing seasonal planting for immediate colour
  • Refreshing decorative gravel or stone borders
  • Redefining overgrown planting beds
  • Shaping established trees and shrubs
  • Introducing suitable low-maintenance planting
  • Cleaning existing patios and pathways
  • Improving the appearance of entrances and front gardens

We consider the property, intended presentation, timescale and budget before recommending work.

Our aim is to achieve an attractive, well-presented result that makes appropriate use of the existing garden.
