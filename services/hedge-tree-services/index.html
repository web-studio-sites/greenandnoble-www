At Green & Noble, we understand the importance of well-maintained trees and hedges in creating beautiful, safe and inviting outdoor spaces.

From carefully shaped ornamental hedges to specialist tree surgery, we provide a comprehensive service that combines practical expertise, professional equipment and exceptional attention to detail.

Whether you're looking to improve natural light, maintain privacy, manage overgrown trees or simply keep your hedges looking their best, our approach is always tailored to your garden and your individual requirements.

Working alongside our fully qualified climbing tree surgeon, Joseph Maybey, we offer professional tree and hedge care services throughout Surrey and the surrounding areas.

Professional Tree Surgery

Trees are often among the most valuable and established features of a garden. Our approach focuses on maintaining their health, natural appearance and suitability for their surroundings.

Our specialist tree surgery services include

  • Crown Reduction

    Reducing a tree's overall height and spread while maintaining a balanced, natural appearance.

  • Crown Thinning

    Selective removal of branches to improve natural light penetration and airflow.

  • Crown Lifting

    Removing lower branches to improve clearance, accessibility and usable garden space.

  • Tree Pruning & Shaping

    Managing growth and maintaining the appearance and structure of established trees.

  • Tree Removal & Dismantling

    Professional removal of unwanted or unsuitable trees, including controlled dismantling in restricted spaces.

  • Deadwood Removal

    Removing dead or damaged branches where appropriate.

  • Ornamental Tree Care

    Specialist pruning and shaping of smaller feature trees and decorative specimens.

Whether managing a mature tree or undertaking more substantial tree surgery, we assess each project individually and recommend the most appropriate approach.

Specialist Tree Surgery

Meet Joseph Maybey

Fully Qualified Climbing Tree Surgeon

We are delighted to have Joseph Maybey working alongside Green & Noble to provide our specialist tree surgery services.

Joseph is a fully qualified climbing tree surgeon, trained in professional tree climbing and aerial tree work using specialist rope systems, climbing techniques and professional equipment.

His expertise allows us to undertake more demanding tree surgery projects, including working at height, crown reductions, controlled tree dismantling and tree management in restricted or challenging locations.

Working closely with Jason and Richard, Joseph helps us deliver a complete and coordinated tree care service, combining specialist expertise with the personal attention, reliability and professionalism that define Green & Noble.

Professional Hedge Cutting & Management

Beautifully maintained hedges can transform the appearance of a property, providing privacy, structure and an attractive backdrop to the wider garden.

At Green & Noble, we provide everything from precision trimming of formal hedges to the reduction and restoration of substantial, overgrown boundaries.

Our hedge care services include

  • Precision Hedge Cutting

    Achieving clean lines, consistent heights and beautifully maintained boundaries.

  • Large Hedge Reduction

    Bringing substantial or overgrown hedges back to more manageable proportions.

  • Formal Hedge Shaping

    Maintaining structured hedges, ornamental shapes and topiary.

  • Boundary Hedge Maintenance

    Managing privacy hedges and established garden boundaries.

  • Hedge Restoration

    Improving the appearance and structure of neglected or uneven hedges.

  • Regular Seasonal Maintenance

    Scheduled hedge cutting to maintain a consistently attractive appearance.

  • Hedge Removal & Clearance

    Removing unwanted hedging and associated green waste.

We understand that every garden is different. Whether the objective is a perfectly maintained formal hedge or a softer, more natural appearance, we take care to achieve a finish that complements the surrounding landscape.

Our Work

Tree & Hedge Work

A Considered Approach to Tree & Hedge Care

At Green & Noble, we believe that responsible tree and hedge management involves more than simply cutting back growth.

Our objective is to enhance the appearance, safety and usability of your outdoor space while respecting the natural character of established planting.

We consider the condition of each tree or hedge, its surroundings, seasonal requirements, wildlife and any relevant tree protection restrictions before undertaking work.

Where possible, we favour sympathetic pruning and careful management to retain the natural beauty of mature trees and established hedges.

Complete Service & Responsible Waste Removal

We appreciate that tree surgery and hedge maintenance can generate significant quantities of green waste.

As a registered waste carrier, Green & Noble can arrange the responsible removal and disposal of branches, hedge cuttings and associated garden waste as part of the agreed service.

We take pride in protecting the surrounding property and garden features throughout our work and leaving the area clean and tidy on completion.

Residential & Commercial Tree Care

We provide tree surgery and hedge care services for

  • Private residential gardens and larger properties
  • Landlords and property management companies
  • Estate agents preparing properties for sale or letting
  • Care homes and managed residential developments
  • Commercial premises and communal gardens

From one-off projects to regular maintenance programmes, we provide a reliable and professional service tailored to the property.
