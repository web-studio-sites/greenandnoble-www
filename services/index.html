Green & Noble provides a comprehensive range of garden care, design advice, soft landscaping, planting and garden improvement services for residential and commercial clients across Surrey and London.

We work with homeowners, estate agents, landlords, property managers and businesses to create, restore, enhance and maintain attractive, well-presented outdoor spaces.

From thoughtfully planned garden transformations and bespoke planting schemes to fencing, seasonal improvements and ongoing professional maintenance, we combine practical horticultural knowledge, quality materials and careful workmanship with an unwavering commitment to customer satisfaction.

Every garden and outdoor space is different. We take time to understand our customers' requirements, provide considered advice and recommend solutions that balance appearance, practicality, long-term care and budget.
