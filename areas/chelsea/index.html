Chelsea Garden Care

Small Spaces, Carefully Kept

Chelsea gardens are often small, walled and close to the house, which means every border, hedge and planter is on show.

Courtyards, roof terraces and townhouse gardens need regular, careful attention to stay at their best. Green & Noble provides garden maintenance, end-of-tenancy garden clearance and landscape gardening across Chelsea, with an owner-led service from Jason Cooper and Richard Maybey.

We arrive when we say we will, protect the property as we work and leave everything clean and tidy, which matters when access is through the house.

Garden Maintenance in Chelsea

Consistent care keeps a compact garden healthy, in proportion and looking considered all year round.

We tailor each visit to the space, whether that is a planted courtyard, a terrace of containers or a longer townhouse garden.

Our Chelsea maintenance visits can include

  • Pruning and shaping of shrubs, climbers and topiary
  • Hedge trimming and screening care
  • Border weeding, feeding and mulching
  • Container, planter and terrace care
  • Lawn mowing, edging and renovation
  • Seasonal planting and replacement
  • Patio and pathway cleaning
  • Removal of green waste

For Landlords & Letting Agents

End-of-Tenancy Garden Clearance in Chelsea

A neglected courtyard or terrace can make a well-presented flat or house feel tired. A garden reset between tenancies restores it quickly and to a high standard.

We support landlords, letting agents and property managers with clearance, restoration and presentation work, planned around viewings, photography and move-in dates.

A typical garden reset includes

  • Clearance of overgrown planting and leaf build-up
  • Cutting back hedges, shrubs and climbers
  • Weeding paving, borders and gravel
  • Cleaning patios, terraces and pathways
  • Tidying planters and containers
  • Removal of all green waste

We keep healthy, established planting wherever possible and can provide ongoing care so the garden stays presentable throughout the next tenancy.

Chelsea Garden Care Questions

Can you look after roof terraces and courtyard gardens?

Yes. Much of our Chelsea work is in smaller spaces, from planted courtyards to terraces of containers, where careful pruning, feeding and watering make the biggest difference.

Our only access is through the house. Is that a problem?

Not at all. We protect floors and surfaces, bag green waste as we go and leave the property clean and tidy when we finish.

Can you work on trees in a conservation area?

Yes. Many Chelsea streets are in conservation areas, where six weeks' notice must normally be given to the council before tree work, and trees with a Tree Preservation Order need consent. We will check this with you before planning any tree work.

How do I get a quotation?

Send us a few photographs through our enquiry form or on WhatsApp, or give us a call. We will discuss what you need and provide a clear written quotation.
